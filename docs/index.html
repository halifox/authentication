Privacy Policy for Authentication

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes the Authentication app maintained by halifox and its handling of data used to generate one-time authentication codes.

Information processed

When you add an authenticator, the app processes and stores its account name (which may be an email address), service name, secret key, and configuration, such as the algorithm, counter, and optional PIN or note. It also stores app preferences on your device. This information is used to manage authenticators and generate codes.

Camera, images, and clipboard

If you choose to scan a QR code, the app uses your camera to read it. You can also select an image containing a QR code or import authenticator data from the clipboard. When you choose to export, the app copies authenticator data, including secret keys, to the system clipboard in plain text. Other software or services with access to that content may be able to read it. Clear the clipboard and protect any copies you make.

Collection and sharing

The app is designed to process authentication data locally. Its current code does not include an app-operated account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or a service that uploads your authenticator data to the developer. The developer does not sell or intentionally share that data. System services, third-party software, and backups you use are governed by their own practices.

Storage, security, and backups

Authenticator records and preferences are stored on your device. The app attempts to encrypt secret keys in its database, but its current encryption uses a key included in the app and must not be treated as protection against someone who can access the app or its data. Depending on your device settings and platform, operating-system backup or device transfer may copy app data outside the device. Exported clipboard contents and any copies are outside the app's control. Use device security and protect backups and exports.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until you delete them in the app or remove the app's local data. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data, subject to your operating system and backup settings. Deleting a record or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete clipboard content, exported copies, or existing system backups; manage those separately. Make sure you have another way to sign in before deleting an authenticator.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the Authentication GitHub issue tracker. Issues are public: do not post secret keys, authentication codes, or other sensitive information. We may update this policy as the app changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.

Authentication 隐私权政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 2 日

本政策说明由 halifox 维护的 Authentication 应用如何处理用于生成一次性认证码的数据。

处理的信息

添加认证器时，应用会处理并保存账户名称（可能是邮箱地址）、服务名称、密钥和认证配置，例如算法、计数器及可选的 PIN 或备注。应用还会在设备上保存偏好设置。这些信息用于管理认证器和生成验证码。

相机、图片和剪贴板

当你选择扫描二维码时，应用使用相机读取二维码。你也可以选择包含二维码的图片，或从剪贴板导入认证器数据。选择导出时，应用会将包含密钥的认证器数据以明文复制到系统剪贴板。能够访问这些内容的其他软件或服务可能读取它们。请清除剪贴板并妥善保护自行保存的副本。

收集和共享

应用被设计为在本地处理认证数据。当前代码不包含由应用运营的账户、云同步、广告、分析功能或向开发者上传认证器数据的服务。开发者不会出售或主动共享这些数据。你使用的系统服务、第三方软件和备份服务受其各自的规则约束。

存储、安全和备份

认证器记录和偏好设置存储在设备上。应用会尝试加密数据库中的密钥，但当前加密使用包含在应用中的密钥，不能视为能够抵御可访问应用或其数据的人员。根据设备设置和平台，操作系统备份或设备迁移可能将应用数据复制到设备之外。导出的剪贴板内容及其副本不受应用控制。请使用设备安全功能并保护备份和导出内容。

保存和删除

记录会保留在设备上，直到你在应用内删除它们或清除应用的本地数据。卸载应用通常会移除本地数据，具体取决于操作系统和备份设置。删除记录或卸载应用不一定会删除剪贴板内容、导出副本或已有系统备份；请单独管理这些副本。删除认证器前，请确保你有其他登录方式。

联系我们与政策更新

如有隐私问题，请通过 Authentication GitHub Issues 联系维护者。Issues 是公开的，请勿发布密钥、验证码或其他敏感信息。我们可能随应用变化更新本政策；上方日期表示最新修订时间。
